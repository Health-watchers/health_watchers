# Running Reports

**Who:** Clinic Admins. **Where:** **Reports** in the main menu (`/reports`).

## Clinic performance overview

1. Choose a period: **This Month**, **Last Month** or **Last 3 Months**. Alternatively, enter custom start and end dates.
2. The summary cards show **New Patients**, **Total Encounters**, **Confirmed Payments** and **AI Summaries** for the period.
3. Charts show patient registrations by month, encounter volume, payment volume by month, revenue by currency, and top chief complaints.
4. To download data for a spreadsheet, click **Export CSV** on the patients, encounters or payments section.

## Custom reports

Open **Reports → Builder** (`/reports/builder`).

1. Start from one of the **Templates**, or pick **Metrics** yourself.
2. Choose a **Date range** (Today, Last 7 days, Last 30 days, Last 90 days, Year to date, or Custom) and a **Chart type**.
3. Give the report a **Report name**.
4. To receive the report by email, choose **Daily**, **Weekly** or **Monthly** instead of **No schedule**.

## Patient satisfaction

**Reports → Surveys** (`/reports/surveys`) shows the results of post-visit satisfaction surveys.

Exported files can contain patient information. Store them securely and delete them when you no longer need them.
