# Inviting Staff

**Who:** Clinic Admins. **Where:** **Settings → Staff** (`/settings/staff`).

## Choosing a role

| Role | Typical person | Can do |
|------|----------------|--------|
| **Clinic Admin** | Practice manager | Everything in the clinic, including staff, settings, reports and billing |
| **Doctor** | Physician | Patients, encounters, prescriptions, lab results, referrals |
| **Nurse** | Nurse | Vitals, lab results, immunizations, triage |
| **Assistant** | Front desk | Appointments, check-in, waitlist |
| **Read Only** | Auditor | View only |

Give each person the lowest role that lets them do their job.

## Invite a staff member

1. Open **Settings → Staff** and click **+ Invite staff**.
2. Enter their **Full name** and **Email address**, and choose a **Role**.
3. Click **Send invite**. You will see *Invitation sent to …*.

The person receives an email with a temporary password. They must change it the first time they sign in.

## Manage an existing account

Find the person using **Search staff** or the role and status filters, then click **Manage**:

- **Save role**: change their role.
- **Force password reset**: email them a new temporary password.
- **Sign out all sessions**: sign them out on every device, for example after a lost phone.
- **Deactivate account**: block access when someone leaves. Use **Reactivate account** to restore it.

You cannot change your own role or access. Ask another administrator. Only a Super Admin can manage administrator accounts.
