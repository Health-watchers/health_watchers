# Handling Patient Data Requests

Patients have the right to see a copy of their records, ask for corrections, and decide how their data is used. Under HIPAA, access requests must be fulfilled **within 30 days**.

## 1. Verify who is asking

Do not release any information until you have confirmed the requester's identity, for example with photo ID or by matching their date of birth and contact details. If someone is acting for the patient, also confirm their authority.

## 2. Request for a copy of records

- **The patient can do it themselves:** in the patient portal, **Records → Download Records as PDF**.
- **Staff on the patient's behalf:** a Doctor or Clinic Admin can export the record in PDF or JSON format. Staff exports require the patient's **data sharing** consent (see step 4). Every export is recorded in the audit log.

## 3. Request to correct information

Open **Patients**, find the patient, and update the incorrect details. The change and who made it are recorded in the audit log. If a clinician disagrees with a correction to clinical notes, ask the doctor to add a note to the record explaining it.

## 4. Changing consent

Open the patient's record and go to the **Consent** tab. For each consent type (**treatment**, **data sharing**, **research**, **marketing**, **AI analysis**), you can click **Grant** or **Withdraw**. Withdrawal takes effect immediately.

## 5. Request to delete data

Medical records usually have a legal minimum retention period, so deletion requests cannot be completed by clinic staff. Record the request and pass it to your compliance officer or Health Watchers support.

## 6. Keep a record

Note the date of the request, how you verified identity, what you provided, and the date you completed it. Administrators can review all access to the patient's record under **Compliance → Audit Logs**.
